# Strand: Cell Biology and Biodiversity

## Sub-Strand 1.1: Introduction to Biology

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of the sub-strand, the learner should be able to:
- Explain the application of Biology in everyday life
- Relate fields of study in Biology to career opportunities
- Illustrate the careers related to fields of study in Biology
- Appreciate the importance of Biology in everyday life

### Key Concepts
- Application of Biology
- Fields of study and careers related to Biology
  - Fields include: Botany, Zoology, Taxonomy, Anatomy, Physiology, Ecology, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Genetics, Parasitology, Microbiology, Entomology

### Key Inquiry Question
- Why is it important to study Biology?
